Placement is identical: same 4 tiers, same GiB, same range counts (393/400/406/129),
same prefill_chunk=4096 and raw_kv_rows=4352. Only compressed_kv_rows scales with ctx
(1026 -> 8194), costing 453.50 -> 1053.75 MiB of context buffers. Zero streaming
messages in either shape -- unlike issues 01/02, which ran under --ssd-streaming on a
single 32 GiB R9700. The two tables are therefore comparable.
